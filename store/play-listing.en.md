## Full description — 1,619 / 4,000

```
Your face is the controller.

FaceArcade turns your phone's front camera into a game pad. Tilt your head, open your mouth, wink, raise your brows — four minigames, no touching the screen at all.

FOUR GAMES

Tilt Runner — Lean your head left or right and the glider follows. Dodge the falling blocks, collect the coins. Three hits and you are done.

Chomp Catch — Move your face under the falling food and open wide to eat it. Bombs and chillies have to be let through with your mouth shut.

Face Mimic — Smile, open wide, wink, tilt your head. Make the face the card asks for before the bar empties. Every round gives you a little less time.

Wink Shooter — A target on the left half means wink your left eye; one on the right means your right. Hit it before it shrinks away.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Face tracking runs entirely inside the app. Your camera feed is never uploaded, never stored and never sent to a server — there is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. Scores are saved on this device only.

BUILT FOR SHORT SESSIONS

Rounds run 45 to 60 seconds. Start a game hands-free by holding your mouth open for a second. Three difficulty levels, and a sensitivity screen where you can watch the live reading from your own face and tune each trigger until it fits you.

SHARE A RUN

Beat your best and FaceArcade renders a card you can send anywhere — the game, the score, your initials, and a link back so a friend can try to beat it.

WHAT YOU NEED

A front camera and even lighting. A bright window behind you is the usual culprit when tracking struggles.

Available in English and Korean.
```

## Privacy policy

**Written and in the repo.** Paste this into Play Console once deployed:

```
https://facearcade.vercel.app/privacy.html
```

The page is [public/privacy.html](../public/privacy.html) — bilingual and
self-contained (no external font or script, so it makes no third-party request of its
own). It covers camera use, the two localStorage keys, share links, Vercel and Google
Fonts as the only third parties, what is not collected, deletion, children and contact.
`vercel.json` also serves it at `/privacy`, so either URL returns 200.
